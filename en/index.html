How to Improve Your Typing Speed

1. Focus on Accuracy First

Beginners do not need to type quickly from the start. Instead, concentrate on pressing the correct keys. Repeated accurate typing can help you develop better typing habits and gradually increase your speed.

2. Learn the Home Row Position

Place your left index finger on the F key and your right index finger on the J key. Most computer keyboards have small raised markings on these two keys to help you find the correct finger positions without looking down.

Place your left-hand fingers on A, S, D, and F, and your right-hand fingers on J, K, L, and the semicolon key on a standard QWERTY keyboard. Try to return your fingers to these positions after pressing other keys.

3. Practice Without Looking at the Keyboard

Gradually reduce the number of times you look at your keyboard. Try to focus on the screen while typing. Although this may feel difficult at first, regular practice can help you remember the positions of the keys.

4. Practice a Little Every Day

Even five to ten minutes of daily practice can help you develop consistent typing habits. Rather than relying only on long practice sessions, make typing a regular part of your daily routine. Take breaks whenever you feel tired.

A Typing Practice Plan for Beginners

If you are new to typing, try following these four stages. Progress at a comfortable pace and focus on developing accurate keyboard movements.

Stage Practice Goal
Stage 1 Learn key positions Accurate typing
Stage 2 Type English words Reduce mistakes
Stage 3 Look at the screen while typing Remember key positions
Stage 4 Practice regularly Improve speed and accuracy

Everyone learns at a different pace. Instead of comparing yourself with other people, aim to improve your own abilities little by little. Small improvements can become meaningful progress over time.

How to Improve Difficult Keystrokes

If you frequently make mistakes with certain letters, take a moment to identify their keyboard positions. Pay particular attention to letters that require fingers you do not normally use very often.

Practice typing English words containing those difficult letters slowly. Once you become comfortable with the correct finger movements, gradually increase your speed. Accuracy should remain your priority.

Why Typing Skills Are Important

Typing is one of the fundamental skills for using a computer effectively. It is useful for writing documents, sending emails, office work, studying, and computer programming.

Accurate typing is especially helpful when learning programming. Programming requires the correct entry of letters, numbers, and symbols. Becoming comfortable with a keyboard can reduce the effort required for typing and help you concentrate on your work.

One Character Equals One Meter

The TAI-SETSUNA speed game uses an original concept in which one correctly typed character represents one meter of travel. Typing speed is converted into virtual movement speed.

Correct characters per second × 3.6 = Speed (km/h)

For example, typing five correct characters per second corresponds to a virtual speed of 18 km/h. Typing ten correct characters per second corresponds to 36 km/h.

This system provides an entertaining way to visualize typing speed and understand your performance through the concept of movement.
